# EchoGraft itch.io Page Copy

## Short Description
Repair a derelict orbital greenhouse by sound: plant, tune, and graft procedural phonoseeds until each resonance garden blooms again.

## Feature List
- Audio-first puzzle-strategy campaign with tutorial, ten chambers, finale, and ending.
- Every sound is synthesized at runtime with Syngen/Web Audio. No samples or external audio assets.
- Deterministic phonoseed DNA and reproducible puzzles.
- Plant, pick up, tune, and graft seeds to match pitch, rhythm, timbre, phase, position, and hazard constraints.
- Keyboard-first controls with captions, text log, semantic UI, volume sliders, reduced-motion, and minimal-visual options.

## Accessibility Notes
EchoGraft is designed for no-vision play. Critical information is communicated through audio and text. The game includes a caption log, screen-reader-friendly status, and independent volume controls. Manual screen reader verification is recommended before treating v0.1.0 as a formal accessibility conformance release.

## Content Notes
No combat gore, no jump scares, no flashing-light mechanics, and no mandatory reflex timing in the main campaign.
