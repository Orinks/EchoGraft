# EchoGraft Manual

## How to Play by Sound
Start a new game, then press Space to scan. The scan pulse reports the chamber heart with pitch, stereo position, and text in the caption log. Move with WASD or arrow keys until the scan says the heart is close. Press Enter to plant the selected phonoseed.

Each chamber asks for a different resonance match. Tune with Minus/Equals or bracket keys. Press Shift to cycle between pitch ratio, pulse rate, brightness, and phase. The status text and caption log report the currently selected seed and tuning value.

Some chambers need more than one planted seed. Press Tab or number keys to change seeds. Press G to graft the first two seeds into a hybrid, then plant the graft where the chamber asks for it.

The main campaign has no mandatory reflex timing. You can scan, read the log, tune, reset, and try again at your own pace.

## Chamber Guide
- Tutorial: learn movement, scan, and planting.
- Heart Echo: repeat scans while moving to the heart.
- Direction: use scan panning and distance.
- Pitch: tune pitch ratio.
- Rhythm: tune pulse rate.
- Timbre: tune brightness.
- Harmony: plant two seeds together.
- Phase: tune phase for cancellation.
- Graft: create and use a hybrid seed.
- Mold: avoid forbidden interval tones.
- Finale: combine all learned mechanics.
